These features are in development and are not available in Ulysses yet.

- **Collaborative applications.** Draft and review NOFO applications with your team in one workspace.
- **Compliance reporting.** Track reporting requirements after award.
- **Audits and public comments.** Keep the records you need to respond to audits and public comments.
- **Reporting.** Report results, use of funds, and project and program status to your board of supervisors, city council, and the public.
- **MCP access.** Connect AI agents to your workspace through the Model Context Protocol.

For what's available today, see the [Introduction](/introduction).
