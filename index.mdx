Ulysses is a civic capital platform for local governments. It tracks projects and programs and the funding behind them, whether that funding comes from the general fund, specialty funds, bonds, loans, or grant awards.

City managers, county executives, and department heads use Ulysses to see how capital projects, such as a bridge reconstruction, and programs, such as homelessness services, are funded, and to work within the constraints of each funding source.

## What you can do with Ulysses

### See and plan your funding

- **See all your projects and programs in one place.** Keep your full portfolio of capital projects and ongoing programs in a single view.
- **See how each one is funded, or not.** Spot funded, partially funded, and unfunded work at a glance.
- **Model funding scenarios.** Plug in different mixes, such as 20% bonds, 10% gas tax, and 70% general fund, and compare the results.
- **Ask Ulysses about your data.** Use trusted AI to ask questions and complete work against the same records your team already uses.

### Find and win awards

- **Find new award funding.** Ulysses tracks Notices of Funding Opportunity (NOFOs) against your project pipeline and matches eligibility to your projects and programs.

### Manage, respond, and report

- **Manage the funding lifecycle.** Handle compliance, reporting, and award drawdowns after the money arrives.
- **Respond to audits and public comments.** Keep the records you need ready when questions come in.
- **Report with confidence.** Show your board of supervisors, city council, and the public the results, how funds were used, and the status of each project and program.

Some features, such as collaborative applications, are not available yet. See [Ulysses' current roadmap](/roadmap).

## Find your way around

| Page | What it's for |
| --- | --- |
| **Discover** | Search available NOFOs, read the latest status of a project, or confirm awards approved by your city council or board of supervisors. |
| [**Projects**](/concepts/overview#projects) | All your projects and programs. Open one to see its department, budget, dates, links, funding status, and capital stack. |
| [**Opportunities**](/concepts/overview#opportunities) | Find the right federal and state Notices of Funding Opportunity (NOFOs) for your projects and programs. |
| [**Awards**](/concepts/overview#awards) | Track award lifecycles, including obligations, drawdowns, reimbursements, and clawback warnings. |
| [**Connections**](/concepts/overview#connections-and-datasets) | Add an external connection to project spreadsheets, verify data access, and manage sync jobs. |
| [**Datasets**](/concepts/overview#connections-and-datasets) | Manage where your external data lives: projects, programs, and awards. |
| [**Workspace**](/concepts/overview#workspace) | Control workspace settings: your team, user permissions, department-level access, and more. |

Join your agency's workspace with an invitation, or sign up to create your own personal workspace. What you can see depends on your role in the workspace.
