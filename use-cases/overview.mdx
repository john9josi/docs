Start from a goal you already have. Each section below says what you can do today and links to the matching page. Features that are still in development point to the [roadmap](/roadmap).

## See and plan your funding

- **See all your projects and programs in one place.** Keep your full portfolio of capital projects and ongoing programs in a single view. Open a project to see its department, budget, dates, funding status, and capital stack. See [Projects](/concepts/overview#projects).
- **See how each one is funded, or not.** Spot funded, partially funded, and unfunded work at a glance. Open a project or program and read its funding status and capital stack.
- **Ask Ulysses about your data.** Use trusted AI to ask questions and complete work against the same records your team already uses. Start in [Discover](/interfaces/web-app) or from the [Quickstart](/quickstart).
- **Model funding scenarios.** Plug in different mixes, such as 20% bonds, 10% gas tax, and 70% general fund, and compare the results. This is not available yet. See the [roadmap](/roadmap).

## Find and win awards

- **Find new award funding.** Ulysses tracks Notices of Funding Opportunity (NOFOs) against your project pipeline and matches eligibility to your projects and programs. See which opportunities match a project, and why, in [Match opportunities](/guides/match-opportunities).
- **Pursue grants collaboratively.** Draft and review NOFO applications with your team in one workspace. This is not available yet. See the [roadmap](/roadmap).

## Manage, respond, and report

- **Manage the funding lifecycle.** Handle compliance, reporting, and award drawdowns after the money arrives. Follow obligations, drawdowns, reimbursements, and clawback warnings on [Awards](/concepts/overview#awards).
- **Respond to audits and public comments.** Keep the records you need ready when questions come in. This is not available yet. See the [roadmap](/roadmap).
- **Report with confidence.** Show your board of supervisors, city council, and the public the results, how funds were used, and the status of each project and program. This is not available yet. See the [roadmap](/roadmap).

## Bring work in and automate

- **Add a project or program.** Register new work so Ulysses can match funding to it. See [Create a project or program](/guides/create-a-project).
- **Bring in your spreadsheets.** Connect external sources and sync them into projects, programs, and awards. See [Connections and datasets](/concepts/overview#connections-and-datasets).
- **Automate your work.** Read and update your workspace from scripts and AI agents. See the [CLI](/interfaces/cli).
