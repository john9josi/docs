Projects and programs are the work and services that get funded. Opportunities are funding you could pursue for that work, and awards are funding you've won. Connections and datasets bring in data from outside Ulysses. Everything lives in your workspace.

## Workspace

Your **workspace** is your agency's account in Ulysses, and the boundary for its data and access. It holds your team, user permissions, and department-level access. Everything you see belongs to the active workspace.

## Organizations

**Organizations** are the agencies and entities your workspace works with.

## Projects and programs

**Projects and programs** are the capital work and public services you track. Each one has a department, budget, dates, links, funding status, and a capital stack: the mix of funding sources paying for it. Projects and programs can also carry federal, state, and CIP identifiers.

## Opportunities

**Opportunities** are federal and state Notices of Funding Opportunity (NOFOs). Ulysses scores opportunities against your projects and programs and stores the matches. Each match has a score and the reasons behind it.

## Awards

**Awards** are funding you've won, tied to the projects and programs they pay for. Ulysses tracks each award's lifecycle, including obligations, drawdowns, reimbursements, and clawback warnings. Awards can be added by hand or from a sync.

## Connections and datasets

A **connection** links Ulysses to an external source, such as a project spreadsheet. Ulysses verifies access and runs sync jobs to keep the data current.

Synced data lands in **datasets**, where you manage how it maps to projects, programs, and awards.

## Activity

**Activity** is a feed of recent changes across your portfolio.
