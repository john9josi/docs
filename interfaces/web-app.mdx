The web app is the main way to work in Ulysses: browsing your portfolio, checking funding status, matching opportunities, and asking questions. For scripts, CI, and AI agents, see the [CLI](/interfaces/cli).

## Sign in

Open [app.tryulysses.com](https://app.tryulysses.com) and sign in with your organization's identity provider. If your agency doesn't use single sign-on, sign in with the email and password from your invitation.

If your email is associated with more than one workspace, you can switch between them in the product. What you can see depends on your role in the active workspace.

## Pages

Everything lives under **Portfolio**:

| Page | What it's for |
| --- | --- |
| **Discover** | Search available NOFOs, read the latest status of a project, or confirm awards approved by your city council or board of supervisors. |
| **Projects** | Your projects and programs. Open one to see its department, budget, dates, links, funding status, and capital stack. |
| **Opportunities** | Find the right federal and state Notices of Funding Opportunity (NOFOs) for your projects and programs. |
| **Awards** | Track award lifecycles, including obligations, drawdowns, reimbursements, and clawback warnings. |
| **Connections** | Add an external connection to project spreadsheets, verify data access, and manage sync jobs. |
| **Datasets** | Manage where your external data lives: projects, programs, and awards. |
| **Workspace** | Control workspace settings: your team, user permissions, department-level access, and more. |

## Ask a question

Ask Ulysses a question about your portfolio, such as the funding status of a project. A good answer cites the project or award it's drawing from.
